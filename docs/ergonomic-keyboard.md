# Ergonomic keyboard shortlist

## Requirements

- Comfortable for long typing sessions.
- Fully split, tentable, and preferably concave.
- Several genuinely reachable thumb keys for Space, Ctrl, Shift, Alt, Enter,
  Backspace, and layers.
- Enough physical keys that numbers and common punctuation do not require deep
  layers.
- Programmable onboard so the layout follows the keyboard across Linux,
  Windows, and macOS.

## Current recommendation

The **Glove80** is the best overall fit. It combines 80 physical keys, concave
key wells, adjustable tenting, six thumb keys per hand, and ZMK firmware. A
dedicated thumb-operated symbol layer can expose brackets, braces, operators,
and shifted number symbols without overloading the pinkies.

Suggested thumb responsibilities:

- Left: Space, Ctrl, sticky Shift, navigation layer.
- Right: Enter, Backspace, symbol layer, Alt.

Its halves communicate wirelessly even when the left half is connected by USB.

## Alternatives

1. **Dactyl Manuform 5x6** — potentially the best physical fit when its keywell
   and thumb cluster are generated for the user's hands. It requires more
   research, assembly, and testing than a finished keyboard.
2. **Kinesis Advantage360** — a polished 76-key contoured keyboard. Choose the
   Pro/ZMK model for portable advanced mappings, or the wired SmartSet model
   for connection reliability. Its geometry is fixed and its switches are not
   hot-swappable.
3. **Dygma Defy** — sixteen thumb keys, hot-swappable switches, and extensive
   programmability, but no concave key wells.

Avoid buying solely from specifications. Thumb-cluster reach depends heavily
on hand size, so use a printable layout or a returnable trial before committing.
